NimTube — Pure Client-Side YouTube Studio & Downloader

NimTube is an open-source, client-side YouTube video downloader and audio converter. Download YouTube videos in 4K 60fps, 1440p, 1080p, and convert audio to MP3 directly in your browser without server bandwidth or quality loss.

Key Features

  • Zero Server Bandwidth: All downloads stream directly between your computer and YouTube CDN using your own connection.
  • 4K & 1080p Full Quality: Downloads separate video and audio streams and merges them seamlessly with in-browser WebAssembly FFmpeg.
  • No Throttling (Speed Bypass): Uses 4 concurrent 8 MB range chunk workers to eliminate YouTube stream throttling.
  • Direct-to-Disk Streaming: Streams large video files directly to your storage via File System Access API without bloating RAM.
  • YouTube MP3 Converter: Extract and convert crystal-clear audio to MP3, AAC, or WAV locally.
  • 100% Open Source & Private: No logs, no analytics, no third-party tracking, verified clean on VirusTotal.

NimTube YouTube Downloader — How It Works

Traditional video downloaders route video streams through proxy servers, causing long queues, low speeds, and privacy risks. NimTube runs directly in your browser using modern web standards including WebAssembly, Web Workers, and the File System Access API. With the companion NimTube Bridge extension, you can download any YouTube video directly using your own IP address at maximum bandwidth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NimTube?

NimTube is a high-speed, client-side YouTube video downloader and media converter powered by WebAssembly.

How do I download YouTube videos in 4K or 1080p?

Simply paste any YouTube video link into NimTube, choose your desired resolution (4K, 1440p, 1080p, 720p), and click Download. NimTube muxes video and audio streams directly in your browser.

Is NimTube safe and virus-free?

Yes. The NimTube extension has a 0/64 clean score on VirusTotal and all source code is open source on GitHub.
